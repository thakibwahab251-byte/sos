The Core Problem

During emergencies, people might not be able to unlock their phones, make calls or communicate their location.

  • ✕ Assailants or hostile situations make taking out and typing on a phone dangerous.
  • ✕ Biometric face/fingerprint unlock often fails under physical struggle or stress.
  • ✕ Dialing 911 verbally alerts the attacker and escalates immediate physical danger.
  • ✕ Victims frequently cannot specify their exact street address when fleeing.
✨

Our Innovation

Discreet, multi-modal emergency detection that acts autonomously on hidden cues:

  • ✓ Voice-triggered emergency activation: Continually processes audio with background Whisper/Vosk keyword spotting.
  • ✓ Discreet emergency alert: Silent activation without bright screen flashes, sounds, or visual feedback.
  • ✓ Location sharing with trusted contacts: High-precision real-time GPS telemetry sent to Angel guardians.
  • ✓ Detects predefined distress phrases: Recognizes "someone is following me", "help me", or custom codewords like "pineapple".
  • ✓ Hidden triggers: Physical volume key cadence (Down-Up-Down) or covert calculator disguise.
